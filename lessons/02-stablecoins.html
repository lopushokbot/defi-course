Why Stablecoins Matter in DeFi

Stablecoins are the foundational backbone of decentralised finance — providing the stability needed to unlock powerful yield strategies without exposure to crypto volatility.

What Are Stablecoins?

Stablecoins are the foundational backbone of decentralised finance, providing stability. Unlike volatile cryptocurrencies, they maintain a steady value, typically pegged to the US dollar.

1 stablecoin = 1 US dollar. A “depeg” occurs when 1 stablecoin ≠ $1, causing significant market disruption.

Types of Stablecoins

Stablecoins come in several varieties, each with different mechanisms for maintaining their dollar peg, trade-offs, and risk profiles.

🏦

Fiat-Backed Stablecoins

Backed 1:1 by USD held in banks and treasuries.

  • Examples: USDT (Tether), USDC (Circle), USD1, FDUSD
  • Pros: Simple mechanism, highly liquid, widely accepted
  • Cons: Centralised control, significant regulatory exposure
🔒

Overcollateralised / Decentralised

Issued by locking crypto as collateral at ratios exceeding 1:1.

  • Example: DAI requires ~$1.50 in crypto to issue $1 of DAI
  • Pros: Decentralised governance, transparent operations
  • Cons: Risk of liquidation during market downturns
⚙

Algorithmic Stablecoins

Maintain peg through algorithmic mechanisms or delta-neutral strategies.

  • Examples: USDE, USDS, USDF
  • Pros: Innovative design, bank-independent, implied yield
  • Cons: Higher depegging risk if strategies fail

Yield Opportunities with Stablecoins

Stablecoins unlock a range of yield-generating strategies in DeFi, from conservative lending to higher-risk liquidity provision.

5–10%
Fiat-based Stablecoin Lending
Deposit USDT/USDC on lending protocols like Aave or Fluid.
12–20%
Algorithmic Stablecoin Lending
Higher-yield by depositing USDE on platforms like Aave or Morpho, with higher risk.
10%+
Liquidity Provision
Supply USDT/USDC pairs to Uniswap to earn trading fees plus additional token incentives.

In traditional finance, USD deposits might earn 1–2% annually. DeFi stablecoin strategies offer 5–15× these returns without exposure to crypto volatility.

Essential Tools for Tracking Stablecoins

Staying informed is critical to maximising your stablecoin yields. These platforms help you monitor opportunities and risks in real time.

📊

DeFiLlama

Comprehensive dashboard for monitoring yields across different blockchains and protocols.

  • Compare APYs across protocols
  • Track TVL (Total Value Locked)
  • Discover new yield opportunities
🔍

CoinGecko / CoinMarketCap

Industry-standard trackers for stablecoin health and performance.

  • Real-time peg stability monitoring
  • Market capitalisation and trading volume
  • Historical performance during market stress events
