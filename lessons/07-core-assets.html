Lesson 07

Unlocking DeFi Yield: Core Assets Strategy

Learn why BTC, ETH, and SOL form the foundation of DeFi yield strategies — and how to put them to work through liquidity provision, lending, staking, and wrapped assets.

Why BTC, ETH & SOL Form the Foundation

💹

Highest Liquidity & Volume

These three dominate trading activity and TVL across the entire DeFi ecosystem, ensuring high market efficiency and consistent demand.

📈

Proven Predictability

With the longest track records among major crypto assets, BTC, ETH, and SOL offer a degree of historical predictability, making them reliable cornerstones for investment.

🌐

Universal Adoption

Accepted across virtually every DeFi protocol and exchange, they provide unparalleled interoperability and access to a wide array of yield-generating opportunities.

DeFi Yield Strategies: Practical Applications

Liquidity Provision

  • Pair BTC, ETH, or SOL with stablecoins (e.g., ETH/USDC, SOL/USDT) in decentralised exchanges (Uniswap).
  • Earn substantial fees from swaps and potential additional incentives from protocols.
  • Anticipate APYs ranging from 40–80%, subject to market conditions and pool specificities.
  • Gives you exposure in assets with proven high return potential.

Collateral for Lending Markets

  • These prime assets are universally accepted across leading DeFi lending protocols like Aave and Compound.
  • Deposit your ETH, SOL, or wBTC (2–8% APY) and borrow stablecoins (2–6% APY).
  • Allows you to maintain exposure to your core assets while freeing up capital for more DeFi strategies.
  • This strategy offers flexibility and capital efficiency.

All these strategies will be demonstrated step by step in the upcoming modules.

Staking: What It Is & Why It Matters

🔒

Proof of Stake Mechanism

In PoS blockchains, network validators secure the network by “locking” or pledging their tokens. This validates transactions and creates new blocks.

🏆

Earn Validation Rewards

Validators receive newly minted tokens and transaction fees. These rewards are shared with those who delegate their tokens to them (stakers).

💰

Passive Yield Generation

For holders, staking offers a low-effort way to earn passive income. Typically 3–7% annual yield in native tokens (ETH or SOL).

📈

Hold & Earn

Generate yield while retaining ownership and exposure to your core assets, aligning long-term holding with active participation in the network’s security.

ETH & SOL Staking Opportunities

ETH Staking via Lido

  • Lido is the biggest ETH staking app, with TVL of $38B and audited by the biggest and most reliable companies.
  • Stake your ETH through Lido and receive stETH (liquid staking token) in return.
  • Earn approximately 3–5% in ETH staking rewards while maintaining liquidity.
  • stETH can be deployed in other DeFi protocols (e.g., Aave, Curve) to “stack” multiple layers of yield.

SOL Staking via Jito

  • Jito is the biggest SOL staking app, with TVL of $3B. Like Lido, but for SOL.
  • Stake your SOL natively or use Jito to receive jitoSOL.
  • Typical yields range from 6–8% APR.
  • The Solana ecosystem often provides additional incentives for integrating jitoSOL within its DeFi ecosystem.

Wrapped Assets: Bridging Chains

₿

wBTC: Bitcoin on Ethereum

wBTC is an ERC-20 token representing Bitcoin on Ethereum. It is 1:1 backed by actual BTC, held in secure reserves, enabling Bitcoin’s value within Ethereum’s DeFi ecosystem.

⬡

wETH: Wrapped Ethereum

wETH is an ERC-20 compliant version of Ether. While ETH itself isn’t fully ERC-20 compatible, wETH facilitates its use across DeFi protocols that require the ERC-20 standard.

🔗

Cross-Chain Utility

The primary purpose of wrapped assets is to enable non-native cryptocurrencies to operate across different blockchain networks, significantly expanding yield-generating possibilities.

Wrapped assets make it easier to use core assets across different blockchains.
