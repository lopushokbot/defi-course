Staying Safe in DeFi: Link Security & Yield Strategies

Identify common threats, secure your assets, and build a balanced portfolio that prioritizes safety without sacrificing reasonable returns.

The Critical Balance Between Security and Yield

The promise of high yields in DeFi comes with significant risks — particularly for newcomers. This guide will help you identify common threats, secure your assets, and build a balanced portfolio that prioritizes safety without sacrificing reasonable returns.

Phishing & Link Security: Your First Line of Defence

🚨

The #1 Beginner Vulnerability

Signing malicious transactions on fake sites is the most common way DeFi users lose funds. These sites often perfectly mimic legitimate platforms.

🔗

Essential URL Hygiene

Always double-check URLs (many fake “Aave” or “Uniswap” clones exist). Never click random links from Telegram, Discord, or Twitter DMs. Bookmark official websites in your browser favourites.

🛡

Defensive Tools

Use wallets with built-in phishing warnings like Rabby or MetaMask's security module. Remember: if the yield looks too good to be true, it usually is a scam.

Building a Safer DeFi Portfolio

Protocol Selection Criteria

  • Choose audited protocols (Certik, Trail of Bits) — you can check through DeFiLlama
  • Prefer battle-tested platforms (Aave, Uniswap, Curve, Lido) that are more than 2 years old
  • Check TVL — low TVL signals higher risk, check through DeFiLlama
  • Research community reputation via DeFiLlama & Twitter

Recommended Portfolio Allocation

  • Stablecoin Lending (largest portion)
  • ETH/SOL/WBTC-USDC/USDT pairs
  • ETH/SOL staking
  • Experimental Farms (smallest portion)

Avoid overexposure to any single chain (except established ones like ETH, ARB, BASE, SOL).

Your DeFi Safety Checklist

1

Test Small First

Always deposit a minimal amount to verify a protocol works as expected before committing significant funds.

2

Stick to the Established

Keep the majority of your funds in protocols with proven track records and significant TVL.

3

Track Everything

Use portfolio trackers like DeBank and DeFiLlama to maintain visibility across all your positions.

4

Avoid Yield Mirages

Be extremely skeptical of protocols offering 1,000%+ APYs — these are typically unsustainable.

5

Create Your Pre-Deposit Ritual

Develop a personal security checklist to review before every transaction.

6

Use Only Checked Links

Do not sign transactions from the wallet if you are not sure about the site's authenticity.

Never share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone. No legitimate protocol or support team will ever ask for them.
