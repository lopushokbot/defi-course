Lending Protocols in DeFi: A Primer

Protocols are essentially autonomous, decentralised banks built on smart contracts, eliminating the need for traditional financial institutions.

How Lending Protocols Work

Lending protocols are essentially autonomous, decentralised banks built on smart contracts, eliminating the need for traditional financial institutions.

🏦

Decentralised Banks

Protocols are autonomous, decentralised banks built on smart contracts, eliminating the need for traditional financial institutions.

👥

Suppliers & Borrowers

Users supply assets (USDC, USDT, ETH, etc.) to earn interest, while others borrow assets using collateral, paying interest in return.

⚙

Algorithmic Rates

Interest rates are determined algorithmically based on supply and demand, ensuring efficiency and transparency.

🤖

Fully Automated

The entire process is automated by code, with no human intervention, ensuring trustless and efficient operations.

Why Lending is Important

Lending protocols are a cornerstone of the DeFi ecosystem.

  • Creates liquidity in DeFi, allowing traders and other protocols to borrow instantly.
  • Enables leverage — users can borrow stablecoins against their BTC or ETH holdings.
  • Offers stable yield for lenders, generally less volatile compared to other DeFi farming strategies.
  • Considered safer relative to newer or more experimental DeFi strategies due to their maturity and audited codebases.

Real-Life Examples of Lending Platforms

🔵

Aave

One of the largest and most prominent lending protocols, supporting multiple chains and wide array of assets. TVL $39.5B (biggest in DeFi). Supports 19 chains.

🟢

Compound

Among the oldest and most battle-tested, known for robust and reliable infrastructure. TVL of $2.7B. Supports 10 chains.

🟣

Fluid

Newer, great UI, supporting multiple chains and wide array of assets. TVL of $1.6B. Supports 4 chains.

🟠

Jupiter

Excellent example of lending markets extending beyond Ethereum ecosystem, demonstrating the multi-chain future of DeFi. Supports 1 chain (SOL).

Yield Opportunities & Risks

Yield Opportunities

4–12%
Stablecoins APR
Generally offer 4–12% APR, with potential for higher rates in smaller, less liquid pools.
1–4%
ETH or wBTC APR
Typically yield 1–4% APR for supplying these major assets.
5–8%
Borrowers APR
Usually pay slightly higher rates, which fluctuate based on asset utilisation, but due to ongoing incentives from chains, borrow APY can be smaller than supply APY (looping strategy).

Protocols often offer additional rewards in their native tokens to bootstrap liquidity and encourage participation. You can get incentives in different coins — for example: Native APY (in stablecoin) + 1% in SEAM coin + 0.91% in MORPHO coin.

Risks

Liquidation Risk — If your borrowed asset's price drops significantly, your collateral can be automatically liquidated. Example: you supply 1 ETH worth $3,000 and borrow 2,500 USDC. If ETH falls to $2,500, the smart contract will liquidate your position.

Interest Rate Volatility — Rates can experience sudden spikes during periods of high market stress. Usually for small amounts of time.

Smart Contract Hacks — Despite audits, protocols are vulnerable to exploits. At the beginning, use highest TVL DApps.
